Zihao Zhao 赵梓豪

Ph.D. student in Information Sciences
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I study how LLM agents reason together—and how to make their coordination reliable and efficient.

I am advised by Hao Wang at UIUC. My research focuses on multi-agent reasoning, learned orchestration, and uncertainty estimation. Previously, I pursued doctoral studies at Rutgers and received my B.Eng. in Automation from Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s Zhiyuan Honors Program.
